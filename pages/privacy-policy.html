2.0 Lead Generation Data

2.1 The Basis We Have For Processing Your Personal Data

This section applies to personal and professional business data collected by V-Marketers for the purpose of supplying you with high-quality products, content, and services as you request, to send important notices, and for internal purposes such as auditing, data analysis, and research to provide you with the information you need to make informed technology purchasing decisions.

2.2 How Long Do We Keep Your Personal Data

We retain your personal data for the duration of your business relationship with us, and we hold your information only as long as necessary for each purpose we use it unless we have obtained your consent to use it for another or similar purpose.

2.3 What Information Do We Collect

V-Marketers collects data about you that is limited to the kind of information that can be found on a typical business card: first name, last name, job title, employer/company name, work address, work email, and work phone number. In some cases, we may ask you to provide additional professional information such as the size of the company you work for and industry type.

To enhance and/or update the information that you have provided to us, we may combine it with professional information or personal data that we collect from third-party sources. The personal data that we refer to in this document is the personal and professional information you provide, as well as information that we obtain from third-party sources, both of which we typically combine as one user record.

2.4 How We Collect It

We may collect your personal data when you register to receive any of the products, content, or services offered by V-Marketers or its third-party sponsors (“sponsors”) such as publications, subscriptions, newsletters, memberships, premium content, webcasts, video, white papers, online seminars, conferences, and events. We source prospect data from various tools such as LinkedIn, Company Websites, and Third-party Data Vendors (such as ZoomInfo, etc.). We collect data on our customer’s behalf, and we share data with our customers as per the campaigns given to us.

2.5 What Happens If You Don’t Provide Personal Data?

In order for us to provide the products, content, or services you request, we need your personal data. Therefore, if you do not provide such personal data, we cannot deliver the requested products, content, or services.

2.6 What We Do With The Personal Data We Collect

  • 2.6.1 To Present Offers That May Be Of Interest To You: We may contact you to offer you additional V-Marketers or third-party products, content, or services that may be of interest in a B2B environment.
  • 2.6.2 To Communicate With You: We may use your personal data to send you important notices, such as communications about your transactions, changes to terms, conditions, policies, and/or other internal purposes.
  • 2.6.3 To Deliver What You Request: We use your personal data to provide the products, content, or services you request.
  • 2.6.4 To Improve Our Service: We may anonymize your personal data and use it for auditing, data analysis, and research to improve V-Marketers’s products, content, and services.
  • 2.6.5 For Third Party Sponsored Content and Events: V-Marketers partners with third-party sponsors to make available a large library of content to our users, such as white papers, professional events, and research reports. Your personal data may be shared with the sponsor(s) of the offer to communicate with you regarding the offer. Once shared, the sponsor’s privacy policy applies.

5.0 Use of ‘Cookies’ / Cookie Policy

Cookies improve your experience. We use cookies to help you access this Website faster and more efficiently. Cookies used by V-Marketers identify you as a unique user by means of a tracking ID. The cookie itself is anonymous and does not contain any Personal Data, except when such information has been supplied by you (e.g. submitting a form).

Essential for the functioning of our website, fault-free registrations, security, and session management.

5.3 Performance/Statistics Cookies

8.0 California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) Notice

This notice applies to California consumers in accordance with the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA). Under the CCPA, California consumers have the right to:

  • Request disclosure of the categories and specific pieces of personal data collected.
  • Request deletion of personal data collected about the consumer.
  • Request to opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal data.

V-Marketers does not sell personal data for monetary consideration. If you wish to submit a Do Not Sell or Deletion request, visit our Opt-Out & Preferences Center or email support@v-marketers.com with "CCPA Request" in the subject line.
