V-Marketers is a specialized B2B lead generation agency. We recognize that privacy is a critical aspect of business relationships. This policy describes how we collect, use, and safeguard the information of our clients and the prospects we engage with on their behalf.

1

Information We Collect

In the course of providing lead generation services, we collect various types of data from professional sources and interactions:

Professional Contact Data

Full names, business email addresses, professional phone numbers, and office locations.

Employment Details

Job titles, departments, company names, and industry classifications.

Digital Footprints

Publicly available LinkedIn profile URLs, business social media interactions, and professional website contributions.

Technical Data

Cookies, IP addresses, and browsing behavior when you interact with our own digital assets or outreach emails.

2

How We Use Your Information

Data is utilized strictly for professional outreach and relationship building within the B2B ecosystem. We process information based on the legitimate interest of connecting businesses with relevant solutions.

  • check_circle Service Delivery: Identifying and connecting our clients with relevant business partners and prospects through targeted campaigns.
  • check_circle Communication: Responding to inquiries, managing client accounts, and sending necessary service updates or reports.
  • check_circle Analytics & Optimization: Improving our outreach methodology and measuring campaign effectiveness to ensure high-quality interactions.
  • check_circle Legal Compliance: Ensuring adherence to global anti-spam laws (CAN-SPAM, CASL) and professional business regulations.
3

Information Sharing and Disclosure

V-Marketers maintains a strict policy against selling personal data to third-party data brokers. Disclosure of information is limited to the following circumstances:

Primary Recipients

Designated Clients

Data collected specifically for a client's lead generation campaign is shared exclusively with that client as part of our contracted deliverables.

Operations

Service Providers

We utilize verified third-party tools (CRM, Email Service Providers) that help us operate our services, under strict confidentiality agreements and data processing addendums.

Compliance

Legal Requirements

When compelled by law, court order, or government regulation, we may disclose information to the extent required.

4

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to ensure the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of all data processed within our ecosystem.

shield

Our security framework includes end-to-end encryption for data in transit and at rest, multi-factor authentication (MFA) for all internal systems, and regular security audits of our technology stack.

Access to sensitive lead data is strictly restricted to personnel essential to campaign fulfillment through a need-to-know access control model.

5

Data Retention Policies

V-Marketers retains personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including for the purposes of satisfying any legal, accounting, or reporting requirements.

Typically, lead data is retained for the duration of a specific campaign and for up to 24 months thereafter for historical performance analysis and to ensure we do not re-engage prospects inappropriately, unless a shorter period is requested by the data subject or the client.

6

Your Rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Regardless of your geographic location, we honor high-standard privacy rights for all individuals in our database. You have the following control over your information:

Right to Access

Request a comprehensive copy of the data we hold about you at any time.

Right to Rectification

Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete professional information.

Right to Erasure

Request the permanent deletion of your personal data from our systems.

Right to Object

Opt-out of any marketing or professional outreach communications immediately.

7

International Data Transfers

As a global agency, your data may be processed in countries outside of your residence, including the United States, where privacy laws may differ.

We utilize Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and robust data protection agreements approved by the European Commission to ensure your data receives an equivalent level of protection everywhere it is handled by our team or our sub-processors.

8

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our internal practices, technological advancements, or regulatory requirements. We will notify active clients of any significant changes via email before they take effect.

The "Last Updated" date at the top of this page indicates the most recent revision.
